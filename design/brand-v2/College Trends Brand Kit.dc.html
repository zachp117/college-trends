01 · Logo

The wordmark

Two stacked lines of Schibsted Grotesk ExtraBold, tracked tight. The blue rule and mono descriptor come from data-table labeling and mark the brand as a reference source. All logo files are outlined vectors — no font needed.

Square, ruled, quiet

07 · Voice

Plain, sourced, neutral

Let the data talk

State the number, the unit and the source. No rankings, no “best”, no hype.

Write for families

Say “what students pay after aid”, then name the field: “average net price”.

Sentence case

Headlines and buttons in sentence case. All caps only for mono labels. No emoji.

DOGraduates earn a median $58,214 ten years after enrolling.
DON’TGraduates make BANK — one of the best ROI schools out there!
